# Credits

## Tarot card art

The 78 card faces are Pamela Colman Smith's illustrations for the Rider–Waite–Smith deck, first published by
William Rider & Son in 1909. They are in the public domain: in the US because they were published before 1929, and
in countries using life + 70 years because Smith died in 1951. The scans come from
Wikimedia Commons (for example [RWS Tarot 00 Fool.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS_Tarot_00_Fool.jpg)
and [Wands01.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wands01.jpg)). They were resized to 520 px wide and lightly
denoised for the web.

The card back (`CardBacks.jpg`) came with the original project and its source is unknown. See
[SHIPPING.md](SHIPPING.md).

## Astronomy

- Jean Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms* (2nd ed.): solar position (ch. 25) and the periodic terms of the lunar
  longitude (ch. 47).
- E. M. Standish, *Keplerian Elements for Approximate Positions of the Major Planets* (JPL), for Mercury, Venus,
  Earth and Mars.
- Constellation star positions are J2000 coordinates of the named stars, rounded.
